IV and Greeks are Black-Scholes values from the quote mid (MODEL), or the broker's IV when no spot is available (FEED). Spot comes from a live index or cash tick of the underlying (UNDERLYING_SPOT_TOKENS_JSON can name one), else a put-call parity estimate. OI chg counts from the first tick this terminal saw today. Shaded cells are in the money. Dimmed cells are stale.
